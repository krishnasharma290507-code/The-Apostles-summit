About The Summit

Where Words Shape Worlds

The Apostles Summit is Delhi's newest Model United Nations conference — a gathering where the next generation of diplomats, debaters, and decision-makers come together to tackle the world's most pressing challenges through structured debate and diplomacy.

For our Inaugural Edition, we bring together 300 delegates from schools and colleges across the country, representing 100 nations across 7 unique committees — each paired with one of the Seven Temptations.
