Where It All Began

The Apostles Summit × Delhi International Public School

Before the Grand Council of 300 delegates and 100 nations, there was a single, defining collaboration — The Apostles Summit × Delhi International Public School. This inaugural edition is more than a conference; it's proof of concept. That world-class diplomatic debate, thoughtful committee design, and genuine student leadership can all coexist under one roof in New Delhi.

Founded by Krishna Sharma, a 19-year-old BA Political Science Honours student who entered the MUN circuit and quickly realised the circuit had become predictable — committees running on autopilot, delegates reading scripts, and the real fun of intellectual challenge disappearing. The Apostles Summit was born from a simple question: what would the MUN we actually want to attend look like?

Our partnership with Delhi International Public School gives the founding Secretariat the stage, the delegates, and the live event experience to shape everything that will become the backbone of The Apostles Summit. Every protocol, every committee structure, every piece of delegate care that you'll experience in October 2026 was shaped by what we learned together.

We carry that legacy forward — with gratitude to Delhi International Public School for being the first to believe in the vision.
